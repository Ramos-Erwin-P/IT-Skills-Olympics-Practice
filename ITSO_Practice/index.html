About ITSO

The University of Makati Annual IT Skills Olympics, or ITSO for short, aims to provide students with a platform to demonstrate, enhance, and test their technical knowledge and practical skills in various fields of Information Technology. Through competitive activities and skill-based challenges, the event encourages innovation, critical thinking, problem-solving, and collaboration while promoting academic excellence and preparing students for the demands of the IT industry.

With categories such as Web Design, Networking, Programming, Game Development, Linux Administration, and even eSports - students are provided with a large array of competitions to show off their skills and represent their alma mater.
